Notes. Sample: common sample of the Global Findex 2025 scam module, N = 16,251 respondents in 75 economies; all respondents own a mobile phone, paid in store by phone or card, and have complete controls. Payment frequency is `fin25e3` (1 = weekly, 2 = monthly, 3 = less than monthly). Group columns are means weighted by the Findex sampling weight `wgt`; shares are in percent, age in years. Within-economy columns report the coefficients on the weekly and monthly dummies from a weighted least squares regression of the characteristic on the two dummies and economy fixed effects, with standard errors clustered by economy (t distribution, 74 degrees of freedom); the reference group is less than monthly. Income quintile 5 excludes Lesotho, which has no income data. The Stage 2 rate is computed among contacted respondents who answered the sending question (N = 4,768); the Stage 3 rate uses the Stage 3 sample, which drops contacted respondents with an unknown sending outcome.
